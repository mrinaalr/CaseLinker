Q03: Kill Chain Interventions

Core research question

The Question

Given Q1 and Q2, where can technology, investigation, or enforcement intervene in the lifecycle to disrupt exploitation.

Q3 traces each exploitation type -- grooming, sextortion, production, coordinated enterprise -- as a harm-signature trajectory through offense stages, and identifies where in that trajectory intervention has the most leverage. That analysis, grounded in federal court records, is developed in full in the paper.
