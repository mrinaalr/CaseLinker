The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the gover-
nment would prove them at trial.
Minor Victim 1 was a fourteen-year-old girl who used the Chirpline application.
In June 2020, the defendant first contacted Minor Victim 1 through the Chirpline
application.
The defendant falsely told Minor Victim 1 that he was a
sixteen-year-old boy.
After that first contact, the defendant told Minor Victim 1 to continue their
conversation on Vellum Chat.
On July 1, 2020, the defendant used the disappearing-message feature
on Vellum Chat to ask Minor Victim 1 to send sexually explicit images
of herself.
Minor Victim 1 then sent the defendant three sexually explicit images, as he
had asked.
