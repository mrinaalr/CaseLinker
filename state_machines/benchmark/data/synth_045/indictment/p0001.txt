The Grand Jury charges that, at all times material to this Indictment,
the facts set out below were true.
Title 18, United States Code, Section 2422(b) makes it a crime to use any
facility of interstate commerce to persuade, induce, entice or coerce a
person under the age of eighteen to engage in unlawful sexual activity.
COUNT ONE. It is charged that, on or about May 19, 2019, the accused did make
a request to Jane Doe 1 for sexually explicit pictures.
COUNT THREE. It is further charged that the accused told Jane
Doe 1 that he would hurt her if she disclosed their messages to
anyone.
Upon conviction, any device used to send or receive the material
described in this Indictment is subject to forfeiture.
As used in this Indictment, the term minor means any person who has not
attained the age of eighteen years.
