By way of the disappearing-message feature on Kitebox, the accused on
June 24, 2020 requested that Jane Doe 1 provide him with sexually
explicit visual depictions of herself.
Jane Doe 1 complied by transmitting to the accused two sexually explicit
visual depictions of herself.
Jane Doe 1 next put a block on the account belonging to the accused.
The block having been put in place, the accused registered a new account
under a false name so as to disguise who he was and again sent messages to
Jane Doe 1.
The accused told Jane Doe 1 on June 27, 2020 that, unless she produced more
images, he would forward the ones he had to her friends.
At a later time, the accused uploaded the images to a Kitebox group
chat that had 3 members.
