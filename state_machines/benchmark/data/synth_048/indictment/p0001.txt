The Grand Jury charges that, at all times material to this Indictment, the facts
set out below were true.
Title 18, United States Code, Section 2422(b) makes it a crime to
use any facility of interstate commerce to persuade, induce,
entice or coerce a person under the age of eighteen to engage in
unlawful sexual activity.
COUNT ONE. The Grand Jury further charges that, on or about
June 24, 2020, the accused did request of Jane Doe 1 that she
send sexually explicit visual depictions of herself.
Upon conviction, any device used to send or receive the material
described in this Indictment is subject to forfeiture.
As used in this Indictment, the term minor means any person who has not
attained the age of eighteen years.
