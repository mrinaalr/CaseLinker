By way of the disappearing-message feature on Snapwire, the accused on March 29,
2021 requested that Jane Doe 1 provide him with sexually explicit visual
depictions of herself.
The accused thereafter received three sexually explicit
pictures from Jane Doe 1, as he had requested.
Jane Doe 1 next put a block on the account belonging to the accused.
Once his account had been blocked by Jane Doe 1, the accused, in order to
keep his identity from being known, set up a new account under a different
name and resumed contact with her.
The accused told Jane Doe 1 on April 5, 2021 that, unless she
produced more images, he would forward the ones he had to her
friends.
At a later time, the images were forwarded by the accused to no
fewer than 9 other Snapwire users.
