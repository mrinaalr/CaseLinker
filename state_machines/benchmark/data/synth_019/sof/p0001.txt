The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the
government would prove them at trial.
At the relevant times, Minor Victim 1 was a minor girl who lived
in another state and used Chirpline.
During August 2021, the defendant published a public post on Chirpline asking
whether any young girls wanted to talk.
An undercover agent posing as a thirteen-year-old girl responded to
the post on Chirpline.
After that response, the defendant asked the undercover agent to
send sexually explicit images.
On September 1, 2021, the defendant arranged to meet the
undercover agent at a restaurant.
On September 9, 2021, the defendant drove to the restaurant to meet
the undercover agent.
