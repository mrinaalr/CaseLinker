The accused admits, and the United States agrees, that the facts below
are true and could have been established beyond a reasonable doubt had
the matter gone to trial.
The accused acknowledges that the internet and cellular telephone networks are
facilities of interstate commerce.
At all times material to the offense, Jane Doe 1 was a girl
under the age of eighteen who resided in a different state and
held an account on Chirpline.
The accused located Jane Doe 1 in February 2021 by means of the
public group search feature on Chirpline, which is also where his
initial contact with her took place.
The accused represented to Jane Doe 1 that his age was seventeen, which was
untrue.
Subsequent to that initial contact, the accused instructed Jane Doe 1
that the two of them should carry on their exchange over Orbit
Messenger.
By way of the disappearing-message feature on Orbit Messenger, the accused on March
18, 2021 requested that Jane Doe 1 provide him with sexually explicit visual
depictions of herself.
