The accused admits, and the United States agrees, that the facts below are true
and could have been established beyond a reasonable doubt had the matter gone
to trial.
The accused understands that the offense of conviction requires proof that a
person used a means of interstate commerce to entice a minor.
At all times material to the offense, Jane Doe 1 was a girl under the age of
eighteen who resided in a different state and held an account on Snapwire.
The accused located Jane Doe 1 in October 2021 by means of the public group
search feature on Snapwire, which is also where his initial contact with her
took place.
The accused represented to Jane Doe 1 that his age was seventeen,
which was untrue.
