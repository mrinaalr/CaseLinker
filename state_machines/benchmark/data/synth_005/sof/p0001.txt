The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the
government would prove them at trial.
At the relevant times, Minor Victim 1 was a minor girl who lived in another state
and used Orbit Messenger.
In February 2024, the defendant found Minor Victim 1 through the public group
search feature on Orbit Messenger and first contacted her there.
The defendant told Minor Victim 1 that he was seventeen years old, which
was not true.
After their first conversation, the defendant directed Minor
Victim 1 to move their chat to Snapwire.
On February 15, 2024, the defendant asked Minor Victim 1 to send
sexually explicit images of herself over Snapwire.
In response, Minor Victim 1 sent the defendant two sexually explicit
images of herself.
