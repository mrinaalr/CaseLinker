By way of the disappearing-message feature on Kitebox, the accused on May 5, 2020
requested that Jane Doe 1 provide him with sexually explicit visual depictions of
herself.
The accused thereafter received three sexually explicit pictures from
Jane Doe 1, as he had requested.
Jane Doe 1 next put a block on the account belonging to the accused.
The block having been put in place, the accused registered a new account under a
false name so as to disguise who he was and again sent messages to Jane Doe 1.
The accused told Jane Doe 1 on May 14, 2020 that, unless she produced more images,
he would forward the ones he had to her friends.
The Chirpline application was among those found installed on the phone of
the accused when it was forensically examined.
