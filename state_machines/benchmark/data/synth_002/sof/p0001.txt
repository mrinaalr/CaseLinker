The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that
the government would prove them at trial.
At the relevant times, Minor Victim 1 was a minor girl who lived in
another state and used Orbit Messenger.
In August 2021, the defendant posted a message in a public Orbit Messenger
group seeking to meet a young girl.
An undercover agent posing as a thirteen-year-old girl responded to the
post on Orbit Messenger.
After that response, the defendant asked the undercover agent to send sexually
explicit images.
On September 27, 2021, the defendant arranged to meet the
undercover agent at a restaurant.
On October 12, 2021, the defendant drove to the restaurant to meet the
undercover agent.
Agents arrested the defendant at the restaurant on October 12, 2021.
