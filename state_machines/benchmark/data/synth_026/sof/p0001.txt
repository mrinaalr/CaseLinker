The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the government
would prove them at trial.
At the relevant times, MV-1 was a minor girl who lived in
another state and used Snapwire.
In or around January 2022, DEFENDANT found MV-1 through the
public group search feature on Snapwire and initiated contact
with her there.
DEFENDANT falsely told MV-1 that he was a sixteen-year-old boy.
After their first conversation, DEFENDANT directed MV-1 to move
their chat to Vellum Chat.
On or about February 19, 2022, DEFENDANT used the disappearing-message feature
on Vellum Chat to ask MV-1 to send images depicting herself engaged in sexually
explicit conduct.
MV-1 then sent DEFENDANT three images depicting sexually explicit
conduct, as he had asked.
On or about March 2, 2022, DEFENDANT arranged to meet MV-1 at a hotel.
