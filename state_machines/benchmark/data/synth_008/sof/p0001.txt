The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the government
would prove them at trial.
Minor Victim 1 was a fourteen-year-old girl who used the Vellum Chat application.
In January 2021, the defendant used the public group search feature on
Vellum Chat to find Minor Victim 1 and sent her a first message.
The defendant falsely told Minor Victim 1 that he was a sixteen-year-old
boy.
After that first contact, the defendant told Minor Victim 1 to continue their conver-
sation on Pinehollow.
On February 1, 2021, the defendant used the disappearing-message
feature on Pinehollow to ask Minor Victim 1 to send sexually
explicit images of herself.
Minor Victim 1 then sent the defendant three sexually explicit images, as
he had asked.
On February 11, 2021, the defendant arranged to meet Minor Victim 1 at a
hotel.
