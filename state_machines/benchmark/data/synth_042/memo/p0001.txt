The United States files this memorandum to set out its position
on the sentence to be imposed.
The factors in Title 18, United States Code, Section 3553(a) include the need for
a sentence to reflect the seriousness of the offense and to deter others from
threatening or enticing children online.
A sentence at the high end of the advisory guidelines range is warranted by
what the accused did.
Under the advisory guidelines, a two-level increase applies where an
offense involved the use of a computer to solicit a minor.
Courts have observed that the distribution of images of a child
causes harm each time the images are viewed.
