The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the gover-
nment would prove them at trial.
At the relevant times, Minor Victim 1 was a minor girl who
lived in another state and used Kitebox.
In October 2021, the defendant used the public group search feature on
Kitebox to find Minor Victim 1 and sent her a first message.
The defendant falsely told Minor Victim 1 that he was a sixteen-year-old
boy.
After that first contact, the defendant told Minor Victim 1 to
continue their conversation on Snapwire.
On October 15, 2021, the defendant asked Minor Victim 1 to send sexually
explicit images of herself over Snapwire.
Minor Victim 1 then sent the defendant three sexually explicit
images, as he had asked.
On November 1, 2021, the defendant arranged to meet Minor Victim 1
at a hotel.
