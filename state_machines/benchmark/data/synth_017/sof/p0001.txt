The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and
that the government would prove them at trial.
At the relevant times, Minor Victim 1 was a minor girl who lived in
another state and used Snapwire.
In January 2023, the defendant used the public group search feature
on Snapwire to find Minor Victim 1 and sent her a first message.
The defendant falsely told Minor Victim 1 that he was a
sixteen-year-old boy.
After that first contact, the defendant told Minor Victim 1 to cont-
inue their conversation on Orbit Messenger.
