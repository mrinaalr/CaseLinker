The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that
the government would prove them at trial.
Minor Victim 1 was a fourteen-year-old girl who used the Pinehollow
application.
In July 2019, the defendant used the public group search feature on
Pinehollow to find Minor Victim 1 and sent her a first message.
The defendant told Minor Victim 1 that he was seventeen years old,
which was not true.
After that first contact, the defendant told Minor Victim 1 to continue their
conversation on Snapwire.
On August 22, 2019, the defendant asked Minor Victim 1 to send
sexually explicit images of herself over Snapwire.
Minor Victim 1 then sent the defendant three sexually explicit
images, as he had asked.
On September 11, 2019, the defendant arranged to meet Minor
Victim 1 at a hotel.
