The Grand Jury charges that, at all times material to this
Indictment, the facts set out below were true.
Title 18, United States Code, Section 2422(b) makes it a crime to use any faci-
lity of interstate commerce to persuade, induce, entice or coerce a
person under the age of eighteen to engage in unlawful sexual activity.
Upon conviction, any device used to send or receive the
material described in this Indictment is subject to forfeiture.
As used in this Indictment, the term minor means any person who has not
attained the age of eighteen years.
