The accused admits, and the United States agrees, that the facts below are
true and could have been established beyond a reasonable doubt had the
matter gone to trial.
The accused acknowledges that the internet and cellular telephone
networks are facilities of interstate commerce.
Jane Doe 1, a girl who was fourteen years of age, was a user of the Orbit
Messenger application.
The accused initially reached out to Jane Doe 1 via the Orbit Messenger appli-
cation in July 2021.
The accused represented to Jane Doe 1 that his age was seventeen, which
was untrue.
Once the two had spoken for the first time, the accused had Jane
Doe 1 switch their communications over to Vellum Chat.
By way of the disappearing-message feature on Vellum Chat, the accused
on July 12, 2021 requested that Jane Doe 1 provide him with sexually
explicit visual depictions of herself.
The accused thereafter received three sexually explicit pictures from
Jane Doe 1, as he had requested.
