The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the
government would prove them at trial.
At the relevant times, Minor Victim 1 was a minor girl who lived in
another state and used Snapwire.
In October 2023, the defendant found Minor Victim 1 through the public group
search feature on Snapwire and first contacted her there.
The defendant told Minor Victim 1 that he was seventeen years old, which was not
true.
After that first contact, the defendant told Minor Victim 1 to
continue their conversation on Pinehollow.
On November 11, 2023, the defendant used the
disappearing-message feature on Pinehollow to ask Minor Victim
1 to send sexually explicit images of herself.
Minor Victim 1 then sent the defendant three sexually explicit images, as he had
asked.
On November 22, 2023, the defendant arranged to meet Minor Victim 1 at a
hotel.
