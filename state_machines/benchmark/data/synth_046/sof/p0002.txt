A request that Jane Doe 1 transmit sexually explicit pictures of
herself was made by the accused over Loopit on October 18, 2024.
The accused thereafter received three sexually explicit pictures from Jane Doe
1, as he had requested.
Jane Doe 1 next put a block on the account belonging to the accused.
The block having been put in place, the accused registered a new account under a
false name so as to disguise who he was and again sent messages to Jane Doe 1.
The accused told Jane Doe 1 on October 25, 2024 that, unless she produced more
images, he would forward the ones he had to her friends.
At a later time, the images were forwarded by the accused to no fewer than 3
other Loopit users.
