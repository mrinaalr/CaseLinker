The United States files this memorandum to set out its position on the sentence
to be imposed.
Under the advisory guidelines, a two-level increase applies where an
offense involved the use of a computer to solicit a minor.
It is the position of the United States that Jane Doe 1 was
placed in fear when the accused said he would reveal her images
to her friends.
Courts have observed that the distribution of images of a
child causes harm each time the images are viewed.
The factors in Title 18, United States Code, Section 3553(a)
include the need for a sentence to reflect the seriousness of
the offense and to deter others from threatening or enticing
children online.
