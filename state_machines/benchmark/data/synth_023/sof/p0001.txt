The parties stipulate that the following facts are true and that the
government would prove them at trial.
MV-1 was a fourteen-year-old girl who used the Orbit Messenger
application.

Orbit Messenger to find MV-1 and sent her a first message.
DEFENDANT told MV-1 that he was seventeen years old, which was not true.
After that first contact, DEFENDANT told MV-1 to continue their conver-
sation on Chirpline.

depicting herself engaged in sexually explicit conduct over Chirpline.
MV-1 then sent DEFENDANT three images depicting sexually explicit
conduct, as he had asked.
MV-1 then blocked and stopped responding to DEFENDANT's account.
